Workflow

Admin Creates Request
        ↓
Status = Pending
        ↓
Collector Views Requests
        ↓
Collector Clicks Accept
        ↓
Status = Assigned
Collector Email Saved
        ↓
Appears In My Tasks
        ↓
Collector Marks Collected
        ↓
Status = Completed
        ↓
Appears In Completed Page
